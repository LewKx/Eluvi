# KI-Prompts: Small space, big living

Für jedes Haus 2 Bilder: **Slide 1 = außen**, **Slide 2 = innen (mit Galerie)**.
Format immer **4:5 (Hochformat)**. Prompts auf Englisch funktionieren in allen Apps am besten.

**Wo eingeben (am Handy):** ChatGPT (Bild erstellen), Microsoft Designer / Bing Image Creator,
Ideogram, Leonardo – oder Canva, sobald das KI-Guthaben wieder da ist.

**Trick für gleiche Häuser innen & außen:** zuerst das Außenbild erstellen, dann in derselben
Unterhaltung/App das Bild als Referenz hochladen und den Innen-Prompt dazuschreiben:
„Same house as in this image, interior view: …“

**Stil-Zusatz (an jeden Prompt anhängen):**
`photorealistic architectural photography, natural light, shot on 35mm, high detail, no text, no people, 4:5 portrait`

## 1. Forest Cabin (wie das erste Haus)
**Außen:** Small two-storey modular timber house in a pine forest at golden hour, light vertical larch cladding, large corner window on the upper floor, cantilevered upper module, wooden deck and stepping stones, moss and ferns around.
**Innen:** Interior of a small modular timber house, open living room with double-height ceiling, sleeping loft gallery with glass railing above, light birch plywood walls, large window to the pine forest, cozy sofa, plants, warm evening light.

## 2. Alpine Loft
**Außen:** Compact modern timber tiny house on an alpine meadow with snowy mountains behind, dark charred wood facade, steep gable roof, one huge triangular glass gable, small wooden terrace, early morning light.
**Innen:** Interior of a compact alpine timber tiny house, steep A-shaped ceiling, sleeping gallery under the roof reached by a ladder, huge triangular window with mountain view, wood-burning stove, sheepskin, minimalist Scandinavian furniture.

## 3. Lake Cabin
**Außen:** Small modular wooden cabin on a lakeshore, floor-to-ceiling glass facing the water, flat green roof, wooden jetty, calm misty lake at sunrise, reflections in the water.
**Innen:** Interior of a small lakeside cabin, open plan kitchen and living space, mezzanine loft bed above the kitchen, floor-to-ceiling window with lake view, light oak floor, linen textiles, morning mist outside.

## 4. Urban Cube
**Außen:** Tiny cube-shaped modular house in a city backyard garden, black metal and natural timber facade, large square window, rooftop plants, string lights, dusk with warm interior light.
**Innen:** Interior of a tiny urban cube house, white walls with timber accents, compact kitchen, floating staircase with storage leading to a sleeping gallery, large square window, smart space-saving furniture, warm dusk lighting.

## 5. Container Loft
**Außen:** Shipping container converted into a modern tiny home, clad partly in wood, large glass sliding door, covered wooden deck, located in a vineyard landscape, warm sunset.
**Innen:** Interior of a converted shipping container home, wooden lining, compact kitchen, loft sleeping platform above the entrance, large glass sliding door, bright and airy, plants and natural materials.

## 6. Coastal Studio
**Außen:** Small timber studio house in coastal dunes with tall grass, weathered silver wood cladding, pitched roof, big window facing the sea, soft overcast light.
**Innen:** Interior of a small coastal timber studio, high pitched ceiling, sleeping loft with wooden railing, window seat facing the sea, white and sand tones, rattan and linen.
